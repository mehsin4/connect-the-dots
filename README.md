# Dot by Dot
A tiny connect the dots game about slowing down.
Draw from one numbered dot to the next and watch a flower slowly appear beneath your hand. The line follows your actual movement, so every drawing is a little different.

Why
To just slow down.
It's just a few quiet minutes of connecting dots, one after another, until something beautiful appears. No score, no timer, no pressure.

Features
12 flower puzzles, each made from 20–40 dots
Random puzzle every time you play
Draw with a mouse or touch
The line follows your hand naturally
Finish a puzzle to reveal the flower with a little celebration

Running it
No setup needed.
Just open index.html in your browser.
Or deploy it to GitHub Pages.

Built with
HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, and SVG.
